# Carrier-003G Dark Persistence-Transition Audit

## Question

At a fixed Carrier-003C decision frontier, do available candidates project different persistence transitions when evaluated without changing ranking, admission, or state evolution?

## Source generations kept separate

The audit will not merge historically different implementations.

1. **Carrier capsule projection:** threshold `0.6`, required persistence `2`, and remembered qualifying inventory change.
2. **Observer-Fix projection:** invariant condition, length at least `8`, projected smoothed duality at or above the configured threshold, bridge activation at least `0.15`, consensus at least `50`, and at least two observer symbols from `👁️` and `🧠`.
3. **V4 projection:** the same six-condition shape with observer symbols `👁️`, `🧠`, `qualia`, and `🌟`.
4. **Earlier latch comparison:** projected smoothed duality crosses the threshold without persistence.

## Transition classes

- `0->0`: remains inactive;
- `0->1`: begins persistence;
- `1->0`: resets persistence;
- `1->2`: reaches declaration persistence;
- `2->2`: remains declared.

## Required measurements

- exact projection comparisons against accepted post-step capsule transitions;
- candidate counts by transition class and source generation;
- frontiers containing more than one projected transition;
- which diagnostic components create candidate divergence;
- accepted transition counts and declaration timing;
- exact baseline replay comparison with coefficient zero.

## Completion condition

The dark audit is complete only when the baseline replay is unchanged, accepted projections reproduce recorded post-step state, and candidate-transition divergence is measured across the full Carrier-003C trajectory.
